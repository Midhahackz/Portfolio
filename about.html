The Idea Behind MidFlow

Building Better Minecraft Creators.

MidFlow started with a simple idea: Minecraft creators should have access to better knowledge, better tools, and better ways to create.

01

Why MidFlow Exists

Minecraft is more than just a game.

Minecraft has become a massive creative platform. People build worlds, create videos, experiment with ideas, and build entire careers around it.

But learning how to actually create better Minecraft content can be difficult. There are countless tools, techniques, mods, workflows and creative decisions to understand.

MidFlow exists to make that easier.

Our Direction

Learn. Create. Improve.

MidFlow is being built around the idea that creators shouldn't have to figure everything out alone.

The Future

More Than Thumbnails.

Thumbnail design is only the beginning. The long-term goal is much bigger.

01

Creator Education

Teach Minecraft creators how to understand thumbnails, editing, content creation, tools and the creative process behind them.

02

Creator Tools

Discover and explain useful tools, mods, resources and workflows that make Minecraft content creation easier.

03

Creator Community

Build a place where Minecraft creators can share work, learn from each other and improve together.

04

A Creator Platform

Eventually bring education, tools, resources, services and community together under one platform.

The Vision

From a Minecraft channel to a platform for creators.

MidFlow is starting small. But the goal isn't to stay small. The goal is to become a place where Minecraft creators can learn, experiment, improve and build something of their own.

Right Now

We're Just Getting Started.

Today, MidFlow is focused on Minecraft content creation, thumbnail design, useful creator tools and practical knowledge.

Every project, video and resource is another step toward the bigger vision.
