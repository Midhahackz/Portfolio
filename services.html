Questions

Before You Order.

How many revisions do I get?

Simple includes 1 revision, Standard includes 2 revisions, and Premium includes 3 revisions.

Can I request a specific style?

Yes. You can send references, examples or describe the visual direction you want.

What format will I receive?

Your final thumbnail will be delivered in a high-resolution format suitable for YouTube.

What if my idea doesn't fit a package?

No problem. Contact me with your idea and I'll help determine which package fits it best.
